    Bank¶

    Summary¶

    Bank is a sixteen-button polyphonic gate source. Each button sets one channel of the output.

    Gate buttons¶

    out outputs the retained pattern in order, up to sixteen channels. Expanders can change the output without changing the button states.

    The numbered switch sets the voltage magnitude; ± selects unipolar or bipolar output.

    The lights show stored button states: an on button is bright when selected and dim otherwise; an off button is unlit.

    Expander interactions¶

    Rex gate-pattern transform¶

    Rex gives Bank a movable window onto its gate pattern.

    start selects the first gate; length sets the gate count. The selection wraps around the pattern.

    Inx gate operations¶

    Inx lets you replace, insert, or apply logical AND to Bank's gates.

    Inx inputs are treated as gates: ≥1 V is high, ≤0.1 V is low. Between these thresholds, the previous state is retained.

    Each input on Inx corresponds to a gate position of Bank's polyphonic output. Inx has three modes:

    • Overwrite: gate states from connected inputs replace the corresponding Bank gates.
    • Insert: all gate states from a connected polyphonic input are inserted before the corresponding Bank gate. If insertion would produce more than 16 positions, Bank truncates the result to 16 positions.
    • AND: a high input preserves the corresponding Bank gate, and a low input clears it.

    Overwrite and AND use the first channel of each input. Unconnected inputs leave the corresponding Bank gates unchanged.

    With Rex and Inx together, Bank applies them in physical left-to-right order.

    Outx breakouts and Cut¶

    Outx routes Bank's gates to separate destinations. It can route individual gates and normalled ranges, or remove those gates from Bank's polyphonic output.

    nrm. selects Individual or Normalled; cut selects Copy or Cut.

    • Individual: each connected port outputs the Bank gate corresponding to its position.
    • Normalled: each connected port outputs the positions after the preceding connected port through its own position, including unconnected positions. Positions beyond a shorter result carry Bank's low voltage: 0 V in unipolar mode, or the negative magnitude in bipolar mode.
    • Copy: all positions remain on Bank's output.
    • Cut: connected positions are removed from Bank's output. In Individual mode, the remaining positions close up. In Normalled mode, only positions after the last connected port remain. A port beyond the current result has no effect.

    Patch ideas¶

    Trigger merger with manual mutes¶

    Place Inx on Bank's left, set mode to AND, and connect monophonic trigger signals to its inputs. Bank merges them into one polyphonic signal at out. Turn a Bank button on to pass the corresponding trigger signal, or off to mute it.
